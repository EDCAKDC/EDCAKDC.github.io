    <h2>Computational cancer immunology</h2>
    <p class="lead">
      I work at the intersection of <strong>CAR-T cell therapy, single-cell genomics,
      immune-repertoire analysis, and translational bioinformatics</strong>.
    </p>
    <p>
      My research uses high-dimensional molecular and clinical data to study immune-cell
      states, clonal dynamics, and determinants of therapeutic response. I am particularly
      interested in computational approaches that connect mechanistic biology with
      clinically meaningful questions in cellular therapy and tumor immunology.
    </p>

</div>
