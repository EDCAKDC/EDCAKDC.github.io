My work centers on **computational cancer immunology**, with an emphasis on CAR-T biology and the integration of molecular, cellular, and clinical data.

## CAR-T cell persistence and state evolution

I study how CAR-T cells change over time and how long-lived populations differ from cells observed near peak expansion. My work combines single-cell transcriptomics, immune-repertoire analysis, differential expression, pathway-level interpretation, and longitudinal comparison.

**Representative questions**
- Which transcriptional states characterize exceptionally persistent CAR-T cells?
- How do dominant TCR clonotypes evolve over long follow-up?
- Which cellular programs distinguish CAR-positive and CAR-negative T-cell populations?
- How can computational analysis connect long-term molecular persistence with clinically meaningful biology?

[Publication-associated analysis →](https://github.com/EDCAKDC/GSE311890-CART-analysis)

## Cancer genomics and clinical association

I analyze genomic and clinical datasets to study molecular features associated with response, progression, and treatment-related outcomes. Typical workflows include mutation and VAF analysis, cohort-level genomic visualization, longitudinal comparison, Kaplan–Meier analysis, Cox modeling, and multivariable association testing.

## Single-cell, TCR, and immune profiling

My single-cell work spans QC, integration, clustering, annotation, state comparison, repertoire analysis, regulon inference, and pathway analysis. I also work with high-dimensional flow-cytometry data using unsupervised methods such as FlowSOM and UMAP.

## Transcriptomics and proteomics

I use bulk RNA-seq and proteomic data to study treatment-induced molecular changes and response-associated signals, with emphasis on reproducible differential analysis, pathway interpretation, and harmonization across heterogeneous biological datasets.

## Systems biology

I am exploring constraint-based metabolic modeling as a complementary framework for studying immune-cell metabolism under tumor-microenvironment constraints, including FBA/FVA, perturbation analysis, and expression-constrained modeling.
