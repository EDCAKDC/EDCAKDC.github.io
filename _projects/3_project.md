## Metabolic systems biology

A computational framework for studying how nutrient constraints, gene-expression constraints, and objective selection reshape feasible T-cell metabolic states.

**Methods:** COBRApy · FBA · FVA · reaction/gene knockout · E-Flux · robustness analysis
